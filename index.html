Privacy Policy for PureSave

Effective Date: September 2026

PureSave ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our extension and services collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

PureSave collects non-personally identifiable information required to perform online price comparisons. This includes product GTIN/EAN barcodes, titles, and price data on e-commerce product pages you view.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, home address, credit card numbers, or full browsing history.

2. How We Use Information

Product data is transmitted securely to our comparison server to find cheaper alternatives and return deal links in real time. We briefly process temporary request logs to enforce security limits against automated abuse.

3. Affiliate Disclosure & Third Parties

PureSave contains affiliate links. When you click a deal link provided by PureSave, we may earn a small referral commission from the merchant at no additional cost to you. We do not sell user data to third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@puresave.app.
